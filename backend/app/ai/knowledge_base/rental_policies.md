# DriveEasy rental policies (SAMPLE - replace with your real policies)

## Eligibility
Renters must be at least 21 years old and hold a valid driving licence. Bike renters need a valid two-wheeler licence. A government photo ID is checked at pick-up.

## Pricing and deposit
Prices are per day. Both the pick-up day and the return day count as rental days. A refundable security deposit of Rs 5,000 for cars and Rs 2,000 for bikes is taken at pick-up and returned within 5 working days after the vehicle is returned undamaged.

## Fuel policy
Vehicles are given with a full tank and should be returned full. Missing fuel is charged at the local fuel price plus a small service fee. Electric vehicles are given fully charged and should be returned with at least 20 percent charge.

## Mileage
Each rental includes 200 km per day. Extra kilometres are charged at Rs 8 per km for cars and Rs 3 per km for bikes.

## Cancellation
Free cancellation up to 24 hours before pick-up. Cancelling within 24 hours of pick-up is charged one day of rental. You can cancel from the My Bookings page.

## Late return
Returns up to 1 hour late are free. After that, each extra hour is charged at 10 percent of the daily price, up to a full extra day.

## Damage and accidents
Report any accident or damage immediately. Minor scratches are covered by the deposit. For major damage the renter pays repair costs up to the insurance excess.
