# DriveEasy FAQ (SAMPLE - replace with your real answers)

## How do I book a vehicle?
Create an account, open the Vehicles page, choose a vehicle, pick your dates and click Confirm booking.

## Can I book for someone else?
The person driving must be the registered renter and show their own licence at pick-up.

## Can I take a vehicle to another state?
Yes, within India. Tell us before pick-up if you plan a long trip so we can check the vehicle's service schedule.

## Which vehicle is best for a family trip?
Vehicles with 5 or more seats suit families. For 6 or 7 people, choose a 7-seater MPV.

## Which vehicles are best for city driving?
Hatchbacks and bikes are easiest to park and use less fuel in traffic.

## Do you offer electric vehicles?
Yes. Electric vehicles are listed with Electric as the fuel type on the Vehicles page.

## How do I contact support?
Use the contact details on the website. Support can help with changes that cannot be made online.
